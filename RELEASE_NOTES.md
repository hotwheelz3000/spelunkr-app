## SPELUNKR 1.0

**More like an app**
- **Loading screen:** the icon on purple with a spinner until the first channel is on.
- **No web giveaways:** no text selection, long-press menus, link previews, pinch zoom or grey tap flashes.
- **Offline screen:** "No connection" with a Retry button; it tunes back in by itself when you're online.
- **Screen stays on** while a channel plays.
- **Lock screen and Control Center** show what's playing.
- **Picture in Picture** when you leave the app with a channel playing.

**First build:** spelunkr.tv as a full-screen app, with videos in the page, fullscreen and landscape, AirPlay and sound that keeps going with the screen locked.
